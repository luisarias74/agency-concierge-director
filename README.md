# Agency Concierge Director ⚡️
> **Interpretable Context Methodology (ICM) Specialist**

## What it is
The **Agency Concierge Director** is a folder-based Claude specialist designed to sit at the high-stress intersection of "Deal Closed" and "Onboarding Started." 

It turns messy intake notes into a cold, clinical **Director’s Brief** so agency owners can make informed Go/No-Go decisions.

## Why it wins
Most AI assistants are too "nice." This specialist is an **Operational Shield**. It identifies "ASAP Syndrome," flags vague success metrics, and scores client readiness out of 100 before you ever open a Project Management tool.

## 📂 Folder Map
- `identity.md`: The "Direct & Skeptical" Director persona.
- `rules.md`: Operational constraints (AI Prepares, Human Approves).
- `examples.md`: Reference benchmarks for High, Med, and Low-quality clients.
- `reference/`: The logic engine (Scoring, Risks, Architecture, and Deployment).

## 🚀 Usage
1. Upload this folder to a **Claude Project**.
2. Paste any client intake, sales note, or transcript.
3. Prompt: *"Run the Director's Review on this intake."*
