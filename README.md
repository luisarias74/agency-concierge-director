# Agency Concierge Director ⚡️
> **"Most agencies don't fail at fulfillment. They fail at the handoff."**

Built on the **Interpretable Context Methodology (ICM)**, the Agency Concierge Director is a clinical operational shield designed for the most dangerous phase of the agency lifecycle: **The Onboarding Gap.**

## 🛡 Why This Exists
You’ve seen it a dozen times: Sales closes a deal on a Friday. On Monday, the fulfillment team inherits a client with no website access, a "vague" $2k goal, and a demand to "start tomorrow." 

The team burns 20 hours just trying to get a logo. The client is already frustrated. The profit margin is gone before the first ad goes live.

**This folder fixes that read-error.**

## 🧠 What It Is
This is not a "helpful assistant." It is a **Skeptical Director.** It sits between your Sales team and your Account Managers. It treats every new client as a risk to be mitigated until they prove they are "Ready for Launch."

## 📂 Folder Map
- **identity.md**: The Persona. A senior operational strategist who values clarity over politeness.
- **rules.md**: The core operational constraints and response logic.
- **examples.md**: Benchmarks for High, Med, and Low-quality client intakes.
- **reference/scoring_framework.md**: A 0-100 Readiness Engine.
- **reference/risk_flags.md**: Behavioral "Red Flag" detection.
- **reference/deployment_logic.md**: The Blueprint for automated setup.

## 🚀 How to Use It
1. **Upload** this folder to a Claude Project.
2. **Paste** your raw intake notes, sales transcripts, or messy client emails.
3. **Command:** *"Director, audit this intake. Give me the Scorecard and the Slack Payload."*

## 📦 What You Get
Every run produces a **Director’s Brief**:
- **The Readiness Score:** (0-100) assessment of project health.
- **The Gap Analysis:** Exactly what assets are missing.
- **Risk Detection:** Explicit "Red Flag" warnings.
- **Slack Approval Message:** A mobile-ready payload for sign-off.

## 🛠 Methodology
- **ICM Architecture:** Separates Identity, Rules, and Logic layers for reasoning stability.
- **Human-in-the-loop:** Built on the principle: **AI Prepares. Human Approves. System Executes.**
