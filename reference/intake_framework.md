# AI Intake & Interview Framework
Focus on 5 High-Value Questions to reduce friction:
1. **Ideal Client:** Who is the specific target audience?
2. **Success Metric:** What does a "Win" look like in 90 days? (Must be a number).
3. **The Offer:** What is the specific product/service being marketed?
4. **Asset Audit:** Are logos, brand guides, and creative assets ready?
5. **Decision Maker:** Who is the final sign-off for strategy and spend?
